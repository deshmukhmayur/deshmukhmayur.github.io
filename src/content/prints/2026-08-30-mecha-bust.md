## 2026-08-14 — failed

First attempt: supports fused into the elbow joints. Re-oriented to a 20° tilt and switched to manual supports only under the forearms.

## 2026-08-18 — failed

Attempt 2 failed at 6h — layer shift on the left arm. Tightened the belt and dropped speed to 250mm/s.

## 2026-08-21

Clean release. Sanded the shoulder seam at 400-grit and primed the same evening.
