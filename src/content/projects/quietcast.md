Quietcast streams a personal radio station from a home server, with per-listener volume curves so late-night listening stays late-night quiet. One stream, many listeners, each with their own gentle loudness.

## why it exists

Streaming services all assume everyone hears the same mix at the same volume. For a household of night owls and early birds, per-listener curves turned arguments about volume into a solved problem.
