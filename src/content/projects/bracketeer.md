Bracketeer is a tournament bracket engine with first-class double-elimination support, built for community leagues that outgrew spreadsheet brackets. Seeding, byes, and rematch avoidance are handled by the library; the UI just draws what the engine decides.

## why it exists

Every local league I played in ran its season off a hand-edited spreadsheet, and every season had the same arguments about byes. The engine makes those decisions deterministic and auditable instead.
