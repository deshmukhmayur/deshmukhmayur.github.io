Stillcounts tracked a film-roll inventory: what's in the freezer, what's expired, and what expires soon enough to justify a weekend shoot. Darkroom shot logs lived alongside each roll.

## why it exists

Expired film is a gamble; expired film you forgot about is a wasted gamble. Forecasting expiration dates turned the freezer into something I could actually plan around.
