atlas-tracer takes raw distributed traces from a fleet of services and reassembles them into a single coherent picture of a request's journey — built for teams whose flamegraph was a wall of noise.

why it exists

Sampling dropped the interesting 2% of spans; queries over ClickHouse rebuilt the path deterministically, so a trace view stayed honest under 1% sampling.

atlas-tracer

work● active2024-03 →tech lead github ↗ · demo ↗

atlas-tracer takes raw distributed traces from a fleet of services and reassembles them into one readable flamegraph — a request's whole journey, sampled down to nothing interesting.

why it exists

Sampling dropped the interesting 2% of spans. Rebuilding the path deterministically from ClickHouse keeps the trace view honest even at 1% sampling rates.
