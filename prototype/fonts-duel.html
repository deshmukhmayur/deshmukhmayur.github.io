The mono duel: three faces for the chrome

Spectral is locked for Studies/Soliloquy. Below, the same terminal chrome — tile, filter row, timestamp — rendered in Space Mono, Kode Mono, and Martian Mono. Same content, three voices.

What to look for in each candidate: the titlebar (does the filename read cleanly at small sizes?), the filter row (do the --flag chips stay crisp?), and the pink timestamp line (does the italic-ish color work with that face's rhythm?). Space Mono is quirky with wide spacing; Kode is blocky and techy; Martian is wide and heavy — notice how it dominates. Candidate 4 is the hybrid: JetBrains carries the working text (captions, chips, identify data), Space Mono carries the voice lines — breadcrumbs, titlebars, timestamps.
